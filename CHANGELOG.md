### Added

**A task declares its own interval and its next moment, and the scheduler keeps the time** ([`6f84bc9`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422)). `TaskPolicy::interval` wakes the task with `WakeReason::Timer` every interval from its spawn, at a fixed rate: a round that comes late wakes the task once, and the next tick is the next multiple of the interval. `ProtocolTask::wake_at`, a provided method that returns `None`, is asked after every poll that leaves the task in its row; the moment it gives wakes the task once with `Timer`, dormant or not. The row holds both (`TaskEntry::next_tick`, `TaskEntry::wake_at`), the round fires them before it polls, and `pump` waits no longer than the earliest one. No thread is started. `ProtocolVTable::wake_at_fn()` reads the function; a vtable from `from_raw_parts` gets one that gives no moment. A panic in `wake_at` ends the task as `Failed`, like a panic in `poll`.
- Why: the kit started one `spawn_interval_waker` thread per cycle (`cycles.rs:131`), and a lifecycle had no way to say when its next stage falls, so `stages.rs` ticks the whole lifecycle at the greatest common divisor of all its moments (`Resolved::tick`, `stages.rs:503-515`) and advances a counted clock on every tick (`drive`, `stages.rs:647-656`).
- Before: [0.3.2's only timer is a thread per task](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/53b72c7b1e5ac02c21f1db447076908db4aff9ef/src/scheduler.rs#L356-L377), and [`pump` waits the whole timeout](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/53b72c7b1e5ac02c21f1db447076908db4aff9ef/src/scheduler.rs#L650).
- After: [`interval` in the policy](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/policy.rs#L109-L114), [`wake_at` on the trait](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/task.rs#L88-L95), [the row's two moments](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L91-L96), [`timer_due`, the fixed rate](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L139-L159), [`next_moment` and `fire_timers`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L707-L729), [`pump`'s wait](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L699-L702), [the round](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L755-L762), and [`wake_at` read after the poll](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L986-L994).

**A task gives its own checkpoint, or none** ([`6f84bc9`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422)). The new `Checkpoint` trait is the state a task gives: `save(&self) -> Vec<u8>`, and `restore(bytes) -> Result<Self, String>`, a safe function that may refuse the bytes. `ProtocolVTable::checkpointed::<T>()` builds a vtable that checkpoints through it; an `Err` from `restore` ends the task as `Failed`, the message naming the type and carrying the error. `ProtocolVTable::live::<T>()` builds a vtable for a task whose state is live and gives no checkpoint, and `gives_checkpoint()` tells the two apart. The scheduler never checkpoints a `live` task: the capitulation strategy is not asked about it, its dormancy does not park it, and Shutdown leaves it live in its row. A Checkpoint, or a Capitulate that keeps a stub, from its own poll or as a message, leaves it live (runnable if it is owed a poll) and is returned as an error naming the task and the reason. A Capitulate with no stub needs no checkpoint, and ends it as `Completed`.
- Why: a cycle's state is the daemon's memory, a closure. 0.3.2 required every task to write `checkpoint` and `restore`, so the kit wrote a checkpoint that returns no bytes and a restore that builds a cycle with no body (`cycles.rs:74-80`), which then fails on its first poll (`cycles.rs:59`). The scheduler could save that empty checkpoint and call it one.
- Before: [`checkpoint` and `restore` required on every task](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/53b72c7b1e5ac02c21f1db447076908db4aff9ef/src/task.rs#L48-L60).
- After: [the `Checkpoint` trait and its doctest](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/checkpoint.rs#L83-L134), [`checkpointed`, `live` and `gives_checkpoint`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/task.rs#L244-L265) with [their functions](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/task.rs#L193-L223), [the strategy not asked about a task that gives none](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L946-L947), and [a Checkpoint not taken](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L1024-L1038).
- Proof: [`a_task_that_gives_its_state_resumes_from_it`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L317-L341) (the store holds exactly the bytes `save` gave, and a wake resumes from them), [`bytes_the_task_refuses_end_it_failed_saying_why`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L343-L360), [`a_live_task_is_never_checkpointed`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L362-L409), [`a_live_task_capitulates_without_a_stub`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L411-L420), and the doctest on `Checkpoint`.

### Changed

**`TaskPolicy::dormancy_ttl` is honoured** ([`6f84bc9`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422)). A task that has gone `dormancy_ttl` without a poll (counted from its spawn until its first), with no message, no wake and no poll owed, is checkpointed with `CheckpointReason::Idle` and is dormant; the reclaim log records it. A wake or a message restores it, with `auto_restore_on_wake`, as any dormant task; so do its interval and its `wake_at` moment. A task whose vtable gives no checkpoint is never parked. `pump` waits no longer than the moment a task's dormancy parks it.
- Why: the kit's policies rely on `TaskPolicy::default()` (`cycles.rs:115`), and a lifecycle that sits between stages for hours had no way to leave memory: the field was declared and never read.
- Before: [the field, "Reserved: the scheduler does not read it yet"](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/53b72c7b1e5ac02c21f1db447076908db4aff9ef/src/policy.rs#L101-L102).
- After: [its docs](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/policy.rs#L101-L108), [`parks_at`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L128-L136), and [`park_idle`, run each round after the timers](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/scheduler.rs#L731-L741).
- Proof: [`dormancy_ttl_parks_a_task_with_no_work_and_a_wake_resumes_it`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L249-L280) (for a `Checkpoint` task and a task in the 0.3 form), [`dormancy_ttl_spares_a_task_with_time_left_or_work`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L282-L299), and [`pump_parks_a_task_when_its_dormancy_passes`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L301-L313).

**`ProtocolTask::checkpoint` and `ProtocolTask::restore` have bodies** ([`6f84bc9`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422)). Each panics, naming the task type, so a task under `ProtocolVTable::checkpointed` or `ProtocolVTable::live` need not write them. A task in the 0.3 form keeps its own, and `ProtocolVTable::of` calls them as before. A task under `of` that wrote neither ends as `Failed` when a checkpoint is asked of it, rather than being saved as nothing.
- Before: [required](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/53b72c7b1e5ac02c21f1db447076908db4aff9ef/src/task.rs#L48-L60). After: [with bodies](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/src/task.rs#L49-L72).
- Proof: [`a_checkpoint_the_task_did_not_write_fails_rather_than_saving_nothing`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/6f84bc9edcebc7d46d07d3421c4fa61c47b88422/tests/time_kit.rs#L422-L434); every 0.3 test still passes unchanged.

**`TaskPolicy` has one more field, `interval`.** `TaskPolicy::default()` and every literal written with `..TaskPolicy::default()` build as before; a literal that names every field needs `interval` too. `TaskEntry` has two more public fields, `next_tick` and `wake_at`; the scheduler builds every row, so no caller's code names them.

**No public item removed.** A rustdoc JSON diff of `53b72c7` and `6f84bc9` (nightly 1.97.0, `--output-format json`, public paths, struct fields, enum variants, trait items and inherent methods compared by name and signature) lists none removed. Two items differ: `ProtocolTask::checkpoint` and `ProtocolTask::restore` now have a body (`has_body` false to true); their signatures are the same. The twelve items added are `Checkpoint` with `save` and `restore`, `TaskPolicy::interval`, `TaskEntry::next_tick` and `wake_at`, `ProtocolTask::wake_at`, `ProtocolTaskPtr::wake_at`, and `ProtocolVTable::checkpointed`, `live`, `gives_checkpoint` and `wake_at_fn`.

**The docs name `wake_at` among the task functions whose panic ends the task as `Failed`** (`ProtocolTask`, `Resident::Empty`, `WakerProtocol::finished`, `run_once`, `TaskState::Failed`); `tests/doc_claims.rs` checks the new wording.

### What the kit changes when it moves to 0.4

Not done in this release; the kit stays on 0.3.2 until it moves. `cycles.rs:115` and `:131`: a cycle is spawned with `TaskPolicy { interval: Some(interval), .. }` under `ProtocolVTable::live::<Cycle>()`, and the waker thread and the empty checkpoint (`:74-80`) go. `stages.rs:647-656`: `drive` gives the lifecycle's next moment through `wake_at` instead of ticking at `Resolved::tick`.

**A `ProtocolVTable`'s functions can be read, and a vtable built from them** ([`1971178`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/1971178034a412645966b22e13d660dff95da15c), [`90b9e93`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/90b9e9383ef60580cff10860f73d09cc296818f6)). `poll_fn()`, `wake_fn()`, `checkpoint_fn()`, `restore_fn()`, `destroy_fn()` and `footprint_fn()` read each function; calling one is `unsafe`. `ProtocolVTable::from_raw_parts::<T>(..)`, an `unsafe const fn`, builds a vtable from hand-written functions, recorded as running `T`, and can build a `static`. ### Security

**A task could be run, or freed, through another type's vtable.** In 0.2.x nothing checked the task pointer against the vtable:
- [`spawn_box`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/scheduler.rs#L272-L298) took whatever vtable was registered under the key and paired it with the `Box<T>` it was given.
- [`ProtocolTaskPtr::from_box`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/task.rs#L40-L51) took any `&'static ProtocolVTable`, and both fields of `ProtocolTaskPtr` were `pub`, so a pointer could be built by hand from anything.
- [`ProtocolVTable`'s fields](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/task.rs#L22-L30) were `pub`, so a vtable could mix two types' functions.
- [`Drop`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/task.rs#L81-L88) then calls `destroy_fn` on `data`, from safe code.

```rust
sched.register_vtable(key, &BIG_VTABLE);
sched.spawn_box(key, Box::new(Small(1)), TaskPolicy::default()); // Ok(TaskId { slot: 0, generation: 0 })
sched.run_once(); // p1. UB: constructing invalid value of type &mut Big: dangling reference

drop(ProtocolTaskPtr::from_box(Box::new(Small(1)), &BIG_VTABLE)); // p2. UB: dangling box
drop(ProtocolTaskPtr { data: Box::into_raw(Box::new(0u8)).cast(), vtable: &BIG_VTABLE }); // p3. UB: dangling box
```

Miri stops the first one in [the vtable's `wake_fn`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/task.rs#L96-L98) and the other two in [`destroy_fn`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/task.rs#L109-L112). Now ([`1ca72a5`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f)) a `ProtocolVTable` [records the type it was built for, and its fields are private](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/task.rs#L52-L78). It is built only by [`ProtocolVTable::of::<T>()`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/task.rs#L115-L143), which `derive_protocol_task_vtable!` [now calls](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/task.rs#L249-L255). [`ProtocolTaskPtr`'s fields are private, and `from_box` asserts the vtable is for `T`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/task.rs#L155-L177). [`spawn_box` refuses a key whose vtable runs another type](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/scheduler.rs#L321-L341). - In 0.2.0: `MIRIFLAGS=-Zmiri-strict-provenance cargo +nightly miri test --test mem claim1_` gave `error: Undefined Behavior: constructing invalid value of type &mut TypeA: encountered a dangling reference (going beyond the bounds of its allocation)`, in 0.2.0's `src/task.rs:97`. The probe spawns a `TypeB` under a key registered with `TypeA`'s vtable. The same command with `claim3_` gave the same error in `src/task.rs:94`, for a pair built through the public fields.

**A message could reach a task of another type, and `dispatch` could be called on any pointer.** In 0.2.x [`Envelope::dispatch`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/envelope.rs#L1-L4) was a safe method that took a `*mut ()`, and [`TypedEnvelope` cast it to `&mut T`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/envelope.rs#L29-L33) without knowing what it pointed at. The scheduler [called it on whatever task the message was queued for](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/scheduler.rs#L604-L606), and [`TaskEntry::mailbox` is `pub`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/scheduler.rs#L55), so any `EnvelopePacket` could be queued for any task.

```rust
typed_envelope::<Small, u64>("poke", 7, |t, n| { t.0 = n; Ok(()) })
    .dispatch(std::ptr::null_mut()); // p4. UB: constructing invalid value of type &mut Small: null reference

// `id` is a Small task.
sched.tasks.get_mut(id).unwrap().mailbox.push_back(typed_envelope::<Big, u64>("poke", 7, |t, n| { t.0[7] = n; Ok(()) }));
sched.run_once(); // p5. UB: constructing invalid value of type &mut Big: dangling reference
```

Miri stops both at [the cast in `TypedEnvelope::dispatch`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/envelope.rs#L31). Now [`Envelope` is an `unsafe trait` with `target_type()`, and `dispatch` is an `unsafe fn`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/envelope.rs#L3-L20); [`TypedEnvelope` reports `T`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/envelope.rs#L43-L59), and [the scheduler checks `target_type()` against the task's type before every `dispatch`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/scheduler.rs#L669-L680). On 0.3.0 the first probe does not compile (``error[E0133]: call to unsafe function `waker_protocol::Envelope::dispatch` is unsafe and requires unsafe block``), and the second gets `run_once -> Err("envelope \"poke\" for task TaskId { slot: 0, generation: 0 } targets another task type than ub::Small")`.
- Test: [`a_message_for_another_type_is_refused_not_delivered`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/tests/scheduler.rs#L174-L188).

- Introduced: 0.2.0, [`envelope.rs`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/23872ee6f934867c5bc0f79357128dad77531d94/src/envelope.rs#L1-L34).

### Fixed

**Cancel frees the task.** In 0.2.x the [Cancel arm](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/scheduler.rs#L360-L364) only set `state = Cancelled`. A cancelled task with nothing queued is not runnable, so nothing removed it: its row and its memory stayed until the scheduler was dropped. Now Cancel [calls `finalize_removal`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/scheduler.rs#L422-L428) ([`1ca72a5`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/commit/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f)): the row goes, the task's `cleanup` runs and it is freed, and its checkpoints follow `retain_on_cancel`.
- Proof: spawn a task that counts its drops, cancel it, run 3 rounds (`p6` in the proofs). 0.2.1: `row present = true, state = Some(Cancelled), Job drops = 0`, and the drop only came when the scheduler itself was dropped. 0.3.0: `row present = false, state = None, Job drops = 1`. Test: [`cancel_removes_and_frees_the_task`](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/tests/scheduler.rs#L249-L259).
- Introduced: 0.2.0, the [same arm](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/23872ee6f934867c5bc0f79357128dad77531d94/src/scheduler.rs#L360-L364).
- In 0.2.0: `cargo test --test logic claim4_ -- --nocapture` gave `claim4: present_after_cancel=true state=Some(Cancelled) freed_before_drop=false`, five rounds after the Cancel.

### Changed

- **waker_tables 0.1.4 to 0.3** ([before](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/Cargo.toml#L14-L17), [after](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/Cargo.toml#L15-L17)). In 0.1.4, `waker_from_arc` took any `&'static RawWakerVTable` as a second argument, and [0.2.x passed its own](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/85969ac8e14418b382217102d8602dd3f868e60a/src/wake_ref.rs#L24-L34).

- **`Envelope` is an `unsafe trait`** with `target_type()`, and `dispatch` is `unsafe` (see Security). A hand-written `Envelope` impl has to change.
- **`ProtocolTaskPtr`**: fields private. [`restore_from_bytes` and `footprint_estimate` are safe, and `vtable()` is new](https://github.com/jameshuntt/waker_protocol/blob/1ca72a5803549914aa822c42cbb91705e267875f/src/task.rs#L179-L229). `Drop` no longer checks for null, since a null `data` can no longer be built.

The type check compares `TypeId`, so this name is what its error messages print.
